About

A lab-bench mind for learning and design

I trained in biology and biochemistry, then founded a microbiology program before moving into education. Since 2020 I've worked at Pearson, first in K–12 virtual school enrollment and now running live training for enterprise learners.

Biology taught me to look for the system behind the surface: how parts connect, what feeds back, and what to measure. I bring that lens to product and program work. I translate between editors, subject matter experts, designers, vendors and partners, and I like turning a messy process into something clear, measurable and automated.

I'm drawn to work where biology, design and learning meet: science communication, learning science, biodesign and biomimicry, and technology built to last. I'm equally happy improving something that already works or taking a new idea from discovery to launch.
