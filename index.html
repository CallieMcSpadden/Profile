I'm drawn to work at the intersection of research, design and education. Interactive 3D · Quantum chemistry

Hydrogen as Probability Clouds

Atoms do not exist as solid pieces of matter held together in the static elemental spheres we commonly see modeled. Instead, our atomic portraits exist as merely the probability of electrons being found within a given region, when measured, at any point in time. Move past the line diagrams and free your perspective in this dynamic portrait of the hydrogen atom. Here, each dot represents a possible position of hydrogen's one electron, sampled from its quantum wavefunction. Navigate through the patterns of energy states and zoom to immerse yourself within clouds of atomic possibility in 3D space.
