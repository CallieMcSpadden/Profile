ABOUT

I have a background in biology and biochemistry, including academic research and instruction, as well as full scale program design and management within industrial fermentation. Since 2020 I've worked at Pearson Education, where I've managed expert-delivered learning programs for professional upskilling in AI, technical and human skills.

A background in STEM taught me to look for the system behind the surface. How parts connect, what feeds back, and what to measure are the lenses I bring to product and program work. I translate between editors, subject matter experts, designers, vendors and partners, and I like turning a messy process into something clear, measurable and future-forward.

I'm drawn to work at intersections of science, design and means for growth; science communication, learner cognition, biodesign, and human-centric technology. With an eye for opportunity, I'm equally capable improving something that already works or taking a new idea from discovery to launch.

Interactive 3D · Quantum chemistry

Hydrogen as Probability Clouds

Atomic particles do not exist as static pieces of matter. These charged components instead exist as probabilities of an energy state within a given area at any point in time. Move past the diagrams of lines and dots and explore this rotatable model of the hydrogen atom represented as thousands of points, each one a possible position of the electron, proton or neutron sampled from its quantum wavefunction. Zoom out to explore the classic orbital shapes from 1s to 4f, then zoom 100,000 times in to within the nucleus.
